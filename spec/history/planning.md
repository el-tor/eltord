## Planning resume point

The receiver-choice privacy policy, payment-timing draft, bounded-free descriptor discovery proposal, and Service Circuit Quote (SCQ) architecture are recorded. An SCQ totals selected relay descriptor rates plus disclosed fees, is accepted before service-side paid construction, stays fixed for that circuit, and is paid in one-minute rounds. Quote/bootstrap encoding, relay rate commitments, exposure accounting, and failure/refund details remain open as listed above. Spec one now has a shared renewal architecture and concrete per-hop wire draft, with two-half coordination above. Exporter/security review, initial commitments, local controller and SCQ continuation encodings, cumulative age defaults, free-only renewal and repricing decisions remain tracked in the [implementation TODO](../../TODO.md). Strict El Tor service version agreement is now recorded above: payment handling is mandatory, the initial service version is 1, and the authenticated SCQ confirms an exact prepayment match without automatic downgrade or ordinary-Tor fallback. The introduction-point request/reply transport for prepayment quotes is now recorded above. Fixed-size envelopes, shared response timing, bounded retries, request/reply field inventories, quote signatures inside HPKE replies, and separate request-encryption keys with bounded rotation are now recorded. The version-one cryptographic suite and strict binary encoding rules are now fixed below. Initial field-by-field layouts, exact signature/HPKE domains, and the sizing inventory are now drafted in the initial wire section. The authenticated payment-policy catalog and exact-reference resolution requirements are now recorded above, including immutable accepted terms and the later invoice-verification gate. Client-signed acceptance and round-specific invoice acquisition are now recorded, including durable idempotency and node-settled reservation before paid construction. Authenticated funded/building/join status, client-confirmed handshake readiness and failure-after-funding rules are now recorded. The service-side first minute now starts at first paid relay-round activation, includes construction, and does not synchronize the client half. The candidate 60-second funded-to-setup deadline and two-round startup funding ceiling are now recorded; authenticated READY and bounded already-funded recovery now define setup completion; exact control encoding and recovery/retry limits remain open; exact source-event/status wire, metadata schemas, independent security/privacy review and runtime validation remain required.

A fingerprinting review is also open for combinations of observable traffic behavior, introduction-circuit replacement, descriptor update timing/size, and identifier reuse. The shared spec-one construction already requires equal-size per-hop encrypted payment fields and dummy zero-fee handshake values; their mere presence is not an added onion-service marker. Normal client comparisons must include idle ten-minute circuits and early closure for poor performance. No traffic-correlation finding has been demonstrated by this planning discussion. Standard Tor introduction-point lifetimes are randomized over a default 18–24-hour range; the draft's ten-minute circuit replacement is a design difference, not a demonstrated deanonymization finding. Preserve the distinction between introduction-point lifetime and the lifetime of a circuit connecting to it when reviewing alternatives. The paid renewal proposal changes the former hard cutoff into renewable payment periods when negotiated; its effect on traffic patterns and long-lived-path exposure requires review. The free-only replacement and receiver-choice privacy policies remain unchanged.

Remaining design work includes security/privacy review of the quote return path, its authenticated bootstrap/SCQ and retry-envelope wire encoding, the complete version-1 required-feature profile, initial relay price/start commitment, SCQ continuation messages, and open renewal/lifetime decisions above. The separate paid-circuit replay-protection investigation remains deferred; these specification edits do not implement its fixes.

**References:**

- [HPKE, RFC 9180](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9180.html) specifies the encryption building block and its security limits; the inner signature/delegation and El Tor message bindings above are additional protocol requirements.
- [BOLT 11](https://github.com/lightning/bolts/blob/master/11-payment-encoding.md) defines the Lightning invoice format; the fresh circuit/round/client association above is an El Tor requirement.
- [Tor onion-service overview](https://community.torproject.org/onion-services/overview/) describes descriptor publication, introduction points, and rendezvous.
- [Tor introduction protocol](https://spec.torproject.org/rend-spec/introduction-protocol.html) defines introduction forwarding, delivery acknowledgments, payload constraints, and introduction rate controls.
- [Tor rendezvous protocol](https://spec.torproject.org/rend-spec/rendezvous-protocol.html) defines circuit establishment and rendezvous cookies.
- [BOLT 12](https://github.com/lightning/bolts/blob/master/12-offer-encoding.md) defines offer/invoice negotiation and distinguishes payment knowledge from payer proof. The authorization and funding rules above are proposed El Tor extensions, not guarantees supplied by BOLT 12.
- [LND blinded invoice creation](https://lightning.engineering/api-docs/api/lnd/lightning/add-invoice/) documents `is_blinded` and blinded-path configuration; support depends on the participating wallet implementations.
- [LND payment timeout and cancellation behavior](https://lightning.engineering/api-docs/api/lnd/router/send-payment-v2/) explains why an in-flight payment can still settle after cancellation or the routing timeout.
- [Tor descriptor publication and lookup](https://spec.torproject.org/rend-spec/deriving-keys.html) defines responsible HSDirs, replication, and periodic/time-period-overlap publication.
- [Tor descriptor outer wrapper](https://spec.torproject.org/rend-spec/hsdesc-outer.html) defines certificates, signatures, lifetime, and revision handling.
- [Tor descriptor encryption](https://spec.torproject.org/rend-spec/hsdesc-encrypt.html) describes address-based confidentiality and optional restricted discovery.
- [Tor network parameters](https://spec.torproject.org/param-spec) defines the descriptor size consensus parameter and randomized introduction-point lifetime defaults.
- [Tor padding specification](https://spec.torproject.org/padding-spec/index.html) describes cover traffic and cautions that alternate choices have anonymity implications.
