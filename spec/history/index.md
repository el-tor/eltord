# Historical material

These documents preserve design history, older diagrams and implementation
experiments. They are not current protocol requirements or evidence that the
revised design is deployed. Follow the architecture and technical chapters for
the proposed version-one profile.

Older text may use post-round payment, unconditional ten-minute teardown,
callback timestamps, weak payment proofs or stronger anonymity claims. Those
statements do not override current authorization, renewal, accounting or privacy
requirements. Undated wallet observations need fresh interoperability testing.
