## Tracking rule

For every new or changed specification requirement, add or update a stable TODO item in the same change. Include its spec link, affected projects, dependencies/open decisions, and concrete acceptance checks. Do not mark an item complete until the implementation and relevant verification exist. Record partial status explicitly. Follow [the spec AGENTS.md](spec/AGENTS.md): every new/edited spec requires a privacy/timing assessment covering observers and identities, linkability/retries, size/framing, timing/lifecycle, resource bounds and remaining validation. Record conclusions and uncertainty in the spec/review notes or local commit description; even editorial changes record that protocol behavior is unaffected. Keep design decisions open when they have not been approved; a TODO is not authorization to start deferred work, spend wallet funds, publish, or push.

- [ ] **ET-23 — Implement bounded encrypted introduction-point quote replies.** Projects: `tor`, `eltord`, `chutney`, client bindings. Spec: [quote bootstrap](spec/02_paid_hidden_service.md#quote-bootstrap-through-the-introduction-point). Architecture approved on 2026-10-25; wire design, security review, and implementation remain deferred. Client requests an SCQ through an admitted free introduction circuit; the service uses its existing introduction circuit, selects candidate relays without building a new circuit, and returns the end-to-end encrypted authenticated quote through a relay-owned expiring reply slot. Define capability/delegation metadata, distinct SCQ request/reply handlers/opcodes/directions, reviewed encryption/signatures, canonical framing, unpredictable slot handles bound to both actual circuit instances and the service registration, strict fragment/byte/time/concurrency/CPU/queue limits, and safe controller events. Acceptance: no paid service build or spending before settled reserved funding; no use of ordinary forwarding ACK as quote/payment proof; malformed/forged/wrong-service replies, replay, stale handles/recycled circuit IDs, lost/late/oversized fragments, flooding, and exhausted free budgets handled safely. Decouple transport-slot expiry from quote/payment state and human approval. Retries/status use fresh authenticated transport nonces, reply keys and ciphertext while retaining logical quote idempotency; stable IDs remain encrypted and no quote validity/spending clock is reset. Test cross-introduction ciphertext matching, reply-key substitution, duplicate paid work, and no routing to arbitrary destinations. Depends on ET-01/02/03/04/11/12/13/14/19; extends ET-21 validation. The approved [fixed-size quote-envelope policy](spec/02_paid_hidden_service.md#fixed-size-quote-envelopes) requires common version-wide request/reply capacities, padding and true content lengths inside authenticated encryption, and constant cell/fragment/last-fragment lengths for valid replies including service refusals. Inventory maximum field/crypto/routing sizes before selecting numeric capacities; do not silently grow quotes or embed arbitrarily long invoices. Test visible endpoint lengths and counts, oversized input, fixed-size refusals, and safe admission/timeout/overload exceptions without unbounded cover traffic. The approved [shared response-timing policy](spec/02_paid_hidden_service.md#shared-response-timing) schedules fresh/cached quote results and authenticated service refusals through the same bounded version-wide window with randomized target release independent of the result. A cache hit cannot reply immediately outside the scheduler. Measure computation/queue/transport effects rather than assuming added jitter equalizes distributions; enforce slot/quote/setup/transmission deadlines and aggregate timer/queue/CPU/free budgets. Test expired results, overruns, malformed/unadmitted traffic, overload, fragment pacing, and Tor padding interaction. Exact wire values, cryptographic construction, numeric sizes, and timing-window parameters remain unchosen; client retry/failover timing is the next design discussion.
